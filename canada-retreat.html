The Work

What You Will Experience

We are not bringing you out just to talk. These days are built to work on your salah, your nafs, your leadership, and your body, all at once.

I

Salah & Quran

Slow, focused prayers in congregation. Practical teaching on presence (khushu) and how to carry it back home. Space to make real tawbah and commitments without distraction.

II

Live Expert Sessions

Sessions on marriage, mental health, fatherhood, and responsibility. How to lead at home with mercy and firmness. Become the leader you always wanted to be, inshaAllah.

III

Physical Challenge

Challenges that remind you you are capable of more than your comfort. Outdoor work that builds courage, grit, and trust between brothers.

IV

Brotherhood

Most men have never had one brother who truly knows them. You will leave with several.

This Retreat Is for a Very Specific Type

Be honest with both columns. The right man recognises himself immediately.

This Is For You If

  • You are a Muslim man (21+) who prays, works, and provides, and inside you know you can be more.
  • Your salah has become a tick-box, your distance is hurting your home, and you are tired of doing this alone.
  • You want real brothers who hold you to a higher standard.
  • You are willing to be uncomfortable, honest, and accountable for 4 days and beyond.

This Is Not For You If

  • You just want an Islamic getaway or some hype.
  • You want comfort more than you want growth.
  • You are looking for therapy or clinical treatment. This is coaching and mentorship.
  • You are not prepared to invest time, energy, and money in changing over the coming months.

This Is Not a Typical Retreat

MasterMan retreats are intentionally designed to help men grow in prophetic faith, fitness, and brotherhood. They are meant to be challenging and transformative. Because of the intimate nature of the retreat, space is limited and registration is capped. The MasterMan Canada 2026 Retreat, hosted by Shaykh Abdullah Oduro, takes place in the Blue Mountains north of Toronto. Over four days, brothers come together for physical training, prayer, learning, Quran recitation, reflection, brotherhood, and the outdoors.
