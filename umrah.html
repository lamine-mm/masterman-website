Who Leads

Your Guides

Shaykh Abdullah Oduro

Imam & Guide · Founder, MasterMan

Abdullah Oduro was born in Arlington, Texas and raised in Houston. He converted to Islam in 1997 and in 1999 was accepted to the Islamic University of Al-Madinah, graduating from the faculty of Islamic Law in 2007 and completing his memorization of the Qur'an the following year. He has worked with organizations including Yaqeen and Bayyinah, and served as Imam at the Islamic Center of Coppell in Dallas. He leads the group through worship, learning, and daily reflection.

Shaykh Omar Husain

Guide & Counselor · LPC · Hafiz

Shaykh Omar Husain is a licensed professional counselor who studied Arabic and the Islamic sciences and graduated from Al-Azhar University in Cairo. A hafiz of the Qur'an, his work centers on Muslim men, families, and the inner work most men avoid. He leads the group alongside Shaykh Abdullah Oduro through teaching and daily reflection.
